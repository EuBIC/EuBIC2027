# Full guidelines for EuBIC-MS Hackathon 2027 project proposals

## Preamble

We, EuBIC-MS, adhere to the [FAIR Principles for Research Software and data](https://doi.org/10.15497/RDA00068). FAIR Principles emphasize making data _Findable_, _Accessible_, _Interoperable_, and _Reusable_. A "fresh look" is outlined [here](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389921000362?via%3Dihub). Any code developed during the hackathon projects has to be distributed under a permissive open-source license. Additionally, all participants are expected to adhere to the [EuBIC-MS Code of Conduct](https://eubic-ms.org/about/code-of-conduct/).

## Scope

### Effort level and timing

A hackathon project should be designed to be achievable within approximately four days by a team of around 10 participants. Recognize that participants may have varying levels of coding expertise, so plan accordingly.Consider allocating time for explaining common tools, such as your preferred code repository or any other tools the team will need. Aspects like coding standards, documentation, unit testing, and continuous integration should also be considered. Break down the project into manageable subunits that can be tackled by 2-3 people working in parallel. Additionally, keep in mind that these parts will eventually need to be integrated, so attention to interface design and definition is essential.

### Topics

Hackathon projects can cover a wide range of areas, including but not limited to:

#### Software Tool Development

- Extension of existing bioinformatics tools under the guidance of their original authors
- Development of novel bioinformatics tools to solve open problems

#### Benchmarking of Software Tools/Algorithms

- Compilation of ground-truth/reference datasets
- Collection of reference implementation
- Specification of benchmarking criteria/free parameters
- Implementation of automated benchmarking workflow

#### Development of Data Analysis Workflows

- Definition of formal protocol
- Implementation of analysis workflow using a reproducible & shareable framework (Galaxy, Nextflow, ...)

#### Dataset re-analysis

- (Re)analysis of publicly available proteomics datasets
- Are the original results reproducible?
- Are the results robust or do they suffer from free parameter choices?

## Proposal submission

Hackathon project proposals can be submitted by creating a [discussion on GitHub](https://github.com/EuBIC/EuBIC2027/discussions/new?category=hackathon-proposals). The project proposal submission deadline is 30 June 2026.

Please provide a detailed description of your project proposal. Submissions should at least include the following information:

**Project description:**

- A general abstract of up to 200 words describing the goal of the project and why it is well suited as a community project.
- A (high-level) project plan detailing the work to be conducted. This primarily includes goals and tasks that will be tackled during the event, but we encourage you to also think about a follow-up strategy.

**Technical details:**

- The programming language(s) that will be used.
- (If applicable) any existing software that will be featured.
- (If applicable) any datasets that will be used and their availability.

**Contact information:**

- Your name and contact information.

To ensure the smooth execution of the event, we require that individuals submitting project proposals will be responsible for their projects if selected for inclusion in the meeting program. Submitters must commit to registering for the EuBIC-MS Hackathon 2027 and will act as the primary contact person overseeing the project. This responsibility may involve preparatory tasks before the event, such as gathering and distributing relevant datasets or coding guidelines, as well as coordinating tasks during the event.

[Submit your project proposal now!](https://github.com/EuBIC/EuBIC2027/discussions/new?category=hackathon-proposals)

## Proposal voting

If you find a submitted project proposal of interest, please **indicate your support** for the project(s). You can upvote for your favorite projects or leave comments to the most interesting proposals.

When discussing an extension of a project proposal please make sure to include all relevant information, as mentioned above.

## Project selection

Proposals will be selected at the discretion of the organizing committee based on several factors, including community support (measured by upvotes), scientific merit, and feasibility.
